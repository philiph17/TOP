Cheese Burger

Description:

A classic American favorite featuring a juicy beef patty, melted cheese, and fresh toppings nestled in a toasted bun.

Perfect for backyard barbecues or a quick, satisfying dinner.

Ingredients:

  • Ground beef
  • Cheddar cheese slices
  • Hamburger buns
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato slices
  • Onion slices
  • Pickles
  • Ketchup and Mustard
  • Salt and pepper

Steps:

  1. Shape the ground beef into patties, slightly larger than the buns.
  2. Season both sides of the patties generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a grill or frying pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook the patties for 3-4 minutes per side for medium doneness.
  5. During the last minute of cooking, place a slice of cheese on each patty and cover to melt.
  6. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill or in a toaster.
  7. Spread ketchup and mustard on the bottom bun.
  8. Place the cheesy patty on the bun.
  9. Top with lettuce, tomato, onion, and pickles.
  10. Close with the top bun and serve immediately.

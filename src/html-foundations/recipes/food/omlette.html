Omlette

Description:

A classic, fluffy omlette made with fresh eggs and seasoned to perfection. A versatile dish that works for breakfast, brunch, or a light dinner.

Quick to make and endlessly customizable with your favorite fillings.

Ingredients:

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: Grated cheese, chopped herbs, or sautéed vegetables

Steps:

  1. In a bowl, beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper until well combined.
  2. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium-low heat and melt the butter.
  3. Pour the egg mixture into the pan and let it sit for a few seconds.
  4. Using a spatula, gently push the cooked edges toward the center, allowing the raw egg to flow to the edges.
  5. When the eggs are mostly set but still slightly moist on top, add any optional fillings.
  6. Fold the omlette in half and slide it onto a plate.
  7. Serve immediately while hot.
